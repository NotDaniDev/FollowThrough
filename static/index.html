Photon Spectrum iMessage Guide

Follow Through is integrated with Photon Spectrum (spectrum-ts) using Project ID 683a5e64... on the Shared iMessage Line tier.

🛡️ Why does Photon require an initial text?

Per the official Photon documentation, shared pool lines enforce an anti-spam security policy: agents cannot send unsolicited outbound messages to unknown numbers until an authorized conversation space is opened.

How to connect your phone in 5 seconds:
  1. Tap "Open iMessage" on the banner (or open Messages on your iPhone).
  2. Send the word START or HELLO to +1 (628) 289-4567.
  3. Our live gRPC listener receives your message and immediately authorizes bidirectional delivery!
  4. You can now receive proactive nudges, deadline warnings, and text new commitments anytime.
💡 Alternative for direct outbound: Log into app.photon.codes, go to project "adp", and add your number to the Outbound Allowlist.
